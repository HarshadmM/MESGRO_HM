## Project overview

This design was one of two individualized partial-hand prostheses that I developed and supervised. It was created for a client with brachydactyly and an intact thumb, making use of the movement available in the thumb and shortened digit stubs.

## Mechanism

The prosthesis uses a capstan-drive tendon mechanism. The intact thumb acts as the primary driver, while residual movement of the brachydactyly stubs supplements the input. This approach uses the client's remaining movement as part of the device's actuation.

## Design priorities

- Tailor the mechanism to the client's individual hand anatomy and available movement.
- Use the thumb as the main input to the capstan-driven tendon system.
- Incorporate movement from the remaining digit stubs as a supplementary input.

## My contribution

I developed and supervised this client-specific prosthesis concept, focusing on the capstan drive and tendon mechanism and how it could use the client's available thumb and stub motion.
