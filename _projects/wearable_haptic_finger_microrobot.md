## Overview

This MRes coursework group project explored a wearable finger device for haptic interaction with a simulated microrobot approximately 200 μm in size. The work brought together mechanical design, multi-sensor embedded systems, and a haptic interaction concept using backlash in a PTFE tube.

## Design Concepts

I proposed three wearable-finger concepts:

1. A segmented finger based on TPU.
2. A segmented finger based on PLA.
3. A continuous TPU finger.

The concepts were developed around three shared design principles:

- Preserve function while limiting fatigue during lengthy interventions.
- Keep the design functional and low cost.
- Integrate cable routing and space for a microcontroller.

## My Role and Contributions
